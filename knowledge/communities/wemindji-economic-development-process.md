# Wemindji — Steps to start a business and Economic Development priorities

Source: "Wemindji Economic Development Support" (scanned document from the Cree Nation of Wemindji Economic Development Department, transcribed November 2026). Confirm details with Wemindji's Economic Development Officer (EDO).

## Steps to start a business in Wemindji
1. **Meet the Economic Development Department.** Discuss your idea and the process, and brainstorm together.
2. **Letter of Intent and personal assessment.** With the EDO, draft a portrait of the project (Project Description Form). The EDO gives you a list of consultants, what needs to be done, and the documents needed to confirm eligibility. A commercial lot or rental space may be identified, and environmental issues must be identified.
3. **Chief and Council review.** The EDO sends your Letter of Intent and Project Description Form to the Director General, who submits it to Chief and Council.
4. **Letter of Support.** Chief and Council review the project and issue a Letter of Support. They may temporarily reserve a commercial lot or space in a band-owned building.
5. **Studies (go / no-go).** A feasibility study, business plan, environmental assessment, building inspection or other studies may be needed (3 proposals requested). You can apply for funding for **up to 75% of the study fees** by completing an Application Form.
6. **Land or space.** Request a Band Council Resolution (BCR) and/or a right of superficies for the reserved commercial lot, or a commercial lease in a band-owned building, or a lease in a building owned by another entity or person.
7. **Implementation funding.** You may request funding to implement the project. **Your contribution should be at least 10% of total project cost (equity).**
8. **Training and support.** You may request funding from CHRD (now Apatisiiwin Skills Development) for training, coaching and salary subsidies for yourself or your employees. You may also request aftercare from the Business Services Center for bookkeeping, accounting, legal counsel, training and coaching.
9. **Business permit request.** Submit your business plan to the EDO and request a Business Permit.
10. **Project analysis (go / no-go).** The EDO completes a Project Analysis Form with recommendations, then submits it to the Board of Directors for TDC's approval.
11. **Business permit issued.** The EDO issues the Business Permit, and you're in business.

## Economic Development Department priorities (from its strategic planning session)
1. **Better services and infrastructure for local entrepreneurs and community members.** Clarify roles, improve the department's structure and processes, and work more closely with other Cree Nation of Wemindji departments and with TDC, TBDC and their subsidiaries, partnerships and joint ventures.
2. **Build an entrepreneurial culture.** Create support and resources for businesses at every stage, including programs for youth and women.
3. **Reduce economic leakage and increase spinoffs.** Wemindji is small and remote, so local businesses rely on residents as repeat customers. Some business types need a minimum population to be profitable, so creative solutions are needed to keep spending in the community.
4. **Better communication.** Clearly communicate services, procedures and guidelines, including the Wemindji Entrepreneurial Assistance Fund, and improve communication with TDC, TBDC and within the Cree Nation of Wemindji.
