# Oujé-Bougoumou — Entrepreneur support programs

Source: summary from Oujé-Bougoumou Cree Nation's Director of Economic Development & Marketing (July 2025). Programs may have changed; confirm with the Economic Development office.

Contact: Economic Development & Marketing, Oujé-Bougoumou Cree Nation, 207 Opemiska Meskino, Oujé-Bougoumou QC G0W 3C0.

## Programs for local entrepreneurs
1. **Micro-business Fund:** for small businesses started from home, or pilot projects of a bigger idea (start small, grow once the concept is proven).
2. **Preliminary Fund:** helps local people get a business plan. Pays up to **$10,000** to a consultant of the entrepreneur's choice.
3. **Equity Assistance Fund:** up to **$25,000 grant** to a local proponent starting a business, to build the equity usually required by funders and banks.
4. **Support Program:** a consultant on standby to help local entrepreneurs with challenges or operations, like mentorship with more services.
5. **Inspire Nights:** a local (sometimes outside) entrepreneur shares their experience, to encourage entrepreneurship and help new owners face challenges.
6. **Entrepreneurship Training:** one evening per month, an open session on an entrepreneurship topic with discussion and questions.
7. **Mini-business (youth 10–15):** a **$500 grant** for youth who want to try a mini-project.
8. **Artist Support (pilot):** helps artists use activities, trade shows and conferences to market their work, and connects them with an artist agency to reach buyers.

In discussion (not yet running, as of July 2025): lawyer support on standby for local businesses, and accounting support to help businesses set up systems.
