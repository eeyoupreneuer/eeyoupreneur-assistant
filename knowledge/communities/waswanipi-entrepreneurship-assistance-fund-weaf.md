# Waswanipi Entrepreneurship Assistance Fund (WEAF) — Policies and Guidelines

Source: "WEAF Policies and Guidelines (2016-02-11)", Waswanipi Economic Development Department (WEDD). This is a 2016 document and may have been updated since. Always confirm amounts, equity rules and deadlines with WEDD.

Contact: Waswanipi Economic Development Department (WEDD), Cree First Nation of Waswanipi, 1 Chief Louis R. Gull Street, Waswanipi QC J0Y 3C0 · Tel. 819-753-2587 · wedd@waswanipi.com

## Purpose
WEAF helps members of the Cree First Nation of Waswanipi (CFNW) with financial, professional and technical support for their businesses. Loans and grants can be used to plan or test a business (feasibility), start a business, or consolidate/expand an existing one. It works mainly as a short-term, low-interest loan program for small and medium businesses.

WEDD mission: encourage an entrepreneurial culture that recognizes and sustains the Cree way of life.

## Who can apply
- A CFNW member, at least 18, living in Waswanipi; or a corporation, partnership or other legal entity 51% owned and controlled by such a member.
- Anyone with outstanding debts to the program or the CFNW must agree to a repayment plan.
- Must not have owned a similar business in the past 12 months.
- Cash equity: **10% of total project cost for ages 18–35; 15% for ages 36 and over.**
- Financing mix for total project cost: no more than 50% grants, no more than 50% debt, minimum 10–15% cash equity.
- Must show the expertise, experience, diplomas, permits or licences needed.
- Must agree to training, courses or mentoring recommended by WEDD, and be actively involved in preparing the business plan or feasibility study.
- Must provide security for the loan (guarantee) and sign the Renunciation to Exemption from Seizure.
- Special consideration may be given to non-Cree permanent residents of Waswanipi aged 18+.

The project must:
- Be approved by a Council resolution.
- Be located and operate mainly from CFNW, with required zoning permits, within the Traditional Territory of the CFNW (Category IA, IB, II or III lands).
- Be full-time and for-profit, and consistent with WEDD's mission.

## Financing programs
**Micro-financing**
- For entrepreneurs with little or no equity who want to test an idea and build equity.
- Up to 100% of project costs, maximum **$5,000**.
- 50% loan, 50% forgivable loan (forgiven if monthly payments are kept up).
- A business plan or feasibility study may not be required.

**Professional fees for a business plan or feasibility study (grant)**
- Up to 25% of professional fees, maximum **$5,000** (the study must cost at least $20,000 to get the full $5,000).

**Start-up of a new business**
- Up to 10% of total project cost, maximum **$20,000** (project must cost at least $200,000 to get the full amount).
- 50% loan, 50% forgivable loan.

**Expansion or consolidation of an existing business**
- Same as start-up: up to 10% of project cost, maximum **$20,000**; 50% loan, 50% forgivable.

## Eligible and non-eligible costs
Eligible (not limited to): professional fees (business plan, feasibility study); capital costs (building, fixtures, equipment, office furniture, leasehold improvements, marketing).

Not eligible: refinancing existing debts; personal expenses; costs before the approved start date; projects without a Band Council Resolution; projects that conflict with community by-laws; purchase of shares.

## Terms
- Only complete applications are processed. Decision within 90 days; decisions are final. Incomplete files close after 60 days of inactivity.
- Competitive interest rate; 6-month deferral of principal and interest; no penalty for early repayment. Monthly payments by automatic withdrawal after the first 6 months of operation.
- Successful applicants must: sign an agreement; allow WEDD to share their success story; hold a business bank account; accept regular follow-ups until the loan is repaid; have an accountant or use WEDD's bookkeeping services; start within 12 months of approval; not get more financing within 12 months or until a previous loan is repaid; not transfer the financing to others.
- If the project isn't carried out as described, funds must be returned. Selling assets bought with program funds before the loan is repaid requires reimbursement.
- Someone whose venture failed is excluded from financing for 2 to 7 years, case by case.
- Projects over $500,000 may get special consideration from the Review Committee.

## Payments
- Professional fees grant: 50% at signing, 50% when the final approved study or plan is received.
- Start-up / expansion: 50% at signing, 40% on a progress report, 10% on completion and final report.

## Documents to attach
- Application form
- Band Council Resolution(s) supporting the project
- Proof of equity: bank statement showing 10% (ages 18–35) or 15% (36+) of total project cost
- Résumé of each promoter
- Business plan or feasibility study, with supplier quotes (may not be needed for micro-financing)
- Financial statements for the past 2 years (existing businesses)
- Operating budget and cash flow
- Confirmation of acceptance from, or proof of application to, all other funding sources

When applicable: permits and licences; diplomas or certificates; letter of authorized agent; partnership agreement; environmental assessment; letters of interest or sales contracts; other supporting documents.

## Deadlines
Quarterly: **June 1, September 1, December 1 and March 1.**
