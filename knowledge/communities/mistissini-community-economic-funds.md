# Mistissini — Community Economic Funds (Cree Nation of Mistissini)

Source: "CNM Brochure 2025", Cree Nation of Mistissini Economic Development (Community Development). Confirm details with the Economic Development office.

Contact: Economic Development, Cree Nation of Mistissini · 187 Main Street, Mistissini QC G0W 1C0 · economic@mistissini.ca · Mon–Fri 8:00–4:30. (The brochure's phone number is a template placeholder, so call the band office for the right number.)

## Who can apply
- Cree beneficiary and member of the Cree Nation of Mistissini.
- Local resident in Mistissini territory.
- At least 18 (16+ for pilot projects).
- No outstanding debt to CNM.
- No conflict of interest with the Economic Development Division or CERB.
- Show expertise or knowledge in the field.
- **Must invest at least 10% equity.**
- **First-time business owners must complete a business management course or mentorship program.**

Corporations: must be at least 51% owned by Cree person(s); the grant is based on the percentage of Cree ownership; partnership/shareholder agreements required.

## What can be funded
- New business start-ups: equipment, inventory, marketing
- Expansions: additional equipment, facility improvements
- Innovation and rebranding: product development, marketing materials
- Vehicles: light trucks/passenger vehicles up to $70,000 (registered to the business)
- Professional services: legal, accounting, consulting
- Market testing: pilot project expenses

Not eligible: capital shares, alcohol or controlled substance sales, replacement equipment, previous outstanding debts, expenses incurred before approval.

## Funding opportunities
- **Professional Fees/Services Grant:** up to **$10,000**, covering **75%** of costs for business plans, feasibility studies, legal advice and HR services.
- **Project Costs Grant:** max **$50,000 or 20%** of eligible costs, for start-ups, expansions, innovations and rebranding.
- **Youth Business Grant (ages 18–35):** an additional **$10,000**, for a total max of **$60,000 or 20%** of eligible costs. Youth also get enhanced mentorship and flexible equity requirements.
- **Pilot Project Grant (ages 16+):** max **$10,000 or 20%** of project cost, to test a concept and market viability.
- **Technical programs:** 75% coverage for a professional assessment of an existing business (operating 12+ months).

## Who approves
- Under $15,000: Internal Working Group
- $15,000–$50,000: Community Economic Review Board (CERB)
- Pilot projects: Internal Working Group

## Required documents
1. Finalized business plan
2. Proof of equity (6 months)
3. Credit rating verification
4. Financial statements (for expansions)

## 4 steps to apply
1. **Submit a letter of intent** outlining your concept and goals.
2. **Preliminary review** by the Economic Development Officer to assess readiness.
3. **Business plan development.** Economic Development supports half the cost of a business plan showing feasibility.
4. **Application and review.** All documents go to the CERB, which decides within 30 days.

## Terms and conditions
- Project must start within 90 days of approval.
- Submit annual financial statements; keep proper bookkeeping.
- No ownership transfer for 3 years without approval.
- Comply with monitoring: regular visits, technical help and mentorship, business management training, financial reporting help, and quarterly reviews for pilot projects.
- Grants must be repaid if: the project doesn't start within 90 days, the business is sold or transferred within 3 years, information was false, terms aren't followed, or the project is inactive for a year.
- Default: 5-year ineligibility for grants and loans (including spouse).
