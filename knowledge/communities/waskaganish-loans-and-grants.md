# Waskaganish — Policies of Economic Development on Loans and Grants Programs

Source: Cree Nation of Waskaganish brochure, "Policies of Economic Development on Loans and Grants Programs" (scanned brochure, transcribed November 2026). Confirm current amounts and deadlines with Economic Development.

## Who runs it
- **Grants:** Economic Development Department of the Cree Nation of Waskaganish (Crees of Waskaganish First Nation).
- **Loans:** Nâtâmuh Management.

Both aim to promote economic development, offer alternative financing for business projects, provide technical support, and build a revolving fund for future projects. Eligible entrepreneurs can get loans and/or grants to start a project or continue an existing business.

## Eligibility of the applicant and project
a) Be a member of the Waskaganish First Nation.
b) The project can be carried out on or off Waskaganish territory.
c) The project is supported by the Band Council.
d) Submit a feasibility study and/or business plan, or another document acceptable to Economic Development and Nâtâmuh.
e) Have completed a "How to Start a Business" course or an equivalent course deemed acceptable.
f) Agree to be involved in preparing the feasibility study and/or business plan.
g) No outstanding debts to the program.
h) Able to provide **5% cash equity** of the total project cost.
i) Agree to sign the Renunciation to Exemption from Seizure.
j) No funding to purchase shares.
k) Joint ventures or partnerships: applicants must have 51% control and de facto (actual) control of operations.
l) Joint ventures must last at least 3 years.
m) A list of prioritized projects exists; prioritized projects get significantly more consideration.

## Funding programs
**2.1 Grant for professional fees (business plan / feasibility study)**
- Meet with Economic Development first to go over the project.
- Maximum grant of **$15,000** for a feasibility study and/or business plan.

**2.2 Grant**
- Maximum **$10,000 per project**.
- Paid out once all funding agencies have approved the financing.

**2.3 Loan to finance a project**
- Maximum loan **$40,000**.
- Loan is at most **10% of total project cost** (project must cost at least $400,000 to qualify for the full $40,000).
- Total loan + grant cannot exceed **$50,000**.

**2.4 Exceptions**
- Pilot projects: grant up to **$2,000**.
- Youth projects: grant up to **$5,000**.
- Projects over **$800,000** may get special consideration for additional loans and grants.

**2.5 Submission deadlines:** May 15, October 15 and February 15.

## Application process
a) Meet with an Economic Development Department representative before submitting.
b) Submit a letter of intent.
c) A feasibility study may be requested before the business plan.
d) The business plan is reviewed by Economic Development and Nâtâmuh.

## Required documents
a) Proof of equity in cash, kept in a separate bank account until the project starts.
b) Proof of good credit rating.
c) Cash flow projection for the next 2 years.
d) Existing businesses: financial statements for the last 2 years (or 1 year if in business less than 2 years), and an operating budget for the next 2 years.
e) Environmental impact and site assessment, when applicable.
f) Partnerships: partnership agreement.
g) Other documents as needed.

## Loan repayment and credit
- Payments by automatic withdrawal.
- First 6 months: no payments.
- Second 6 months: interest-free; only capital is repaid.
- Repayment period up to 7 years.
- Interest rate: **4%**.
- Management uses all legal means necessary to recover its investment.
- Credit margin / bridge financing may be available, with proper guarantees, while waiting for other funders to disburse.
