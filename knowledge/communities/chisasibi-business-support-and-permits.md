## Chisasibi Business Service Center Inc. (CBSC Inc.)
Mission: advance community-based economic development by strengthening local entrepreneurship, promoting responsible business growth, and supporting sustainable opportunities rooted in Cree ownership, Eeyou values and the long-term prosperity of Chisasibi.

Values: respect for Eeyou values, traditions and lands; entrepreneur-centred service; good governance and accountability; sound business and financial practices; fairness and integrity; community economic benefit.

Works in collaboration with the Cree Nation of Chisasibi (CNC) Economic Development Department. Since July 1, 2022, CBSC uses an application process developed with CNC, the Cree Nation Government (CNG), Eeyou Economic Group (EEG) and CREECO.

### Requirements to apply to CBSC
- Be 18 or older.
- Submit a letter of intent and a market survey.
- Be a Cree beneficiary affiliated with the Cree Nation of Chisasibi, and a JBNQA beneficiary.
- Business must be owned solely by Cree beneficiaries, with majority interest held by a Cree beneficiary affiliated with CNC.
- Have proof of equity.
- No arrears with CNC, CNG, EEG or CREECO, and not in default on any prior loan from CBSC, CNC, CNG, EEG or CREECO.
- Partnerships / joint ventures: each partner submits their own application form (using the same business name).
- No existing file in the past 12 months for the same business idea (except a file extension).
- Other requirements may apply depending on the type of business.

The application form asks for personal information, education, details about any open files or arrears, ownership, whether you have a business plan or feasibility study, and the name and nature of the business. Files with no activity for 12 months are closed (with one month's notice).

### What CBSC does with your application
1. Sends an acknowledgement receipt.
2. Verifies information and opens a file.
3. Presents the file to the Board of Directors.
4. Contacts you with the General Manager's and Board's decision.
5. Presents a plan to you.

### File approval process — what happens once your file is approved
1. **Business plan.** The plan sets the project cost, your financial contribution, and how much CBSC Inc., the Cree Nation Government, Eeyou Economic Group and CREECO may contribute. You work closely with an assigned consultant until the plan is done.
2. **Funding applications.** Some require a Council Resolution. CBSC sends a briefing sheet on your project to Chief and Council, who send the Council Resolution directly to you if approved.
3. **Once funders approve, they'll need:**
   - Local business permit/licence (register with the CNC Economic Development Department)
   - Business bank account
   - Partnership agreement (if applicable)
   - NEQ (Quebec Enterprise Number)
   - A location/lot approved by Council Resolution

If you already have all of the above and need no more help, you only need: a **support letter from CBSC** (confirms the project follows the CNC Business Permit By-law), a **Council Resolution**, **NEQ registration**, and a **local business permit**.

## Cree Nation of Chisasibi business permits
Managed by the CNC Economic Development Department under the **Business Permit By-Law**.

**Contact:** Shelley Herodier, Interim Economic Development Officer · shelleyherodier@chisasibi.ca · 819-855-2878 ext. 576 · Office at CBSC Inc., Commercial Center 2nd floor, 15 Maamuu Road, Suite 203.

### Long-term business permit (local businesses)
- Anyone operating a business on **Category IA land** of the Cree Nation of Chisasibi must get a business permit.
- Permits run January to December each year.
- Fees: **new permit $100; renewal $50.**

### Short-term business permit (merchants from outside Chisasibi)
- Outside merchants must apply to sell in the community.
- Short-term = 1 day to 4 consecutive weeks.
- Fees: **$300 per week of validity** (same for renewal).

### Paying permit fees
Debit at the Administrative Building, or e-transfer to cncemt@chisasibi.ca with "Business Permit" (or "Short-Term Business Permit") in the message.

### Business permit application form
Asks for: business identification (name, address, contact, description of activities, hours), NEQ number, organization type, legal structure, industry type, start-up date, entrepreneur identification and ownership (owners, % ownership, Cree beneficiary status and community), business certifications/licences, equipment inventory, description of premises (with surface in square metres), and specialized human resources.

Documents required:
- Payment of fees under the by-law
- Articles of incorporation
- Joint venture agreement, if applicable (including any agreement on managing daily activities)
- Copy of the lease or deed of purchase for the premises
- Itinerant merchants: copy of the permit issued under the Consumer Protection Act
- Renewals: copy of the current business permit
