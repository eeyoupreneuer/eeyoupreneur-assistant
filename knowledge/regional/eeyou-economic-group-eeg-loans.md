# Eeyou Economic Group / CFDC Inc. (EEG) — Business loans

Sources: EEG description of loan funds (screenshot) and EEG "Loan Application Form" (PDF). Confirm current amounts and requirements with EEG.

EEG offers business loans for start-ups and expansions for Cree entrepreneurs in Eeyou Istchee.

## Loan funds
- **EEG Investment Fund:** repayable business loan for start-up or expansion. Finances up to **40% of total project cost, max $150,000.**
- **Youth Strategy Fund:** for eligible entrepreneurs aged **18–39**, financing up to **$25,000.**
- **Micro Fund:** for smaller start-up needs, up to **$5,000.** Up to 50% may be given as a grant if all program requirements are met and scheduled loan payments are made.

## Loan policies
- Promoter must be Cree and a beneficiary under the James Bay and Northern Quebec Agreement (JBNQA).
- Project must take place in Cree territory.
- Project supported by a Band Council resolution, or an agreement in principle with the local Band Council.
- Complete business plan required.
- Loan must be guaranteed.
- Financial statements required when requesting additional financing or buying an existing business.
- Other documents may be needed, such as Waiver of Exemption (from seizure) and resolutions, environmental assessment approval, and partnership or joint venture agreement.

## Application form asks for
Project name, amount requested, applicant(s) and partners with ownership %, project type (start-up, purchase, expansion, modernization), project description, community, economic activity, applicant's current status, project costs and investors, personal equity (cash and other assets), and jobs created or maintained. The applicant authorizes EEG to check credit and other information. If the applicant doesn't follow up within 30 days of acceptance, the approval can lapse.

## Checklist to include with the application
- Completed loan application
- Full business plan
- Band resolution supporting the project
- Lease agreement or band resolution for the land (lot)
- Curriculum vitae
- Partnership agreement (if more than one person)
- Other supporting documents
