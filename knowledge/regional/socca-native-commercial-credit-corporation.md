## About
SOCCA has financed Indigenous businesses in Quebec since 1992. It offers non-repayable contributions, loans and personalized coaching. Mission: a strategic partner offering financial services and advice tailored to Indigenous clients in the commercial sector.

Eligible clients: First Nations (Anishinaabe, Atikamekw, Eeyou (Cree), Mi'gmaq, W8banaki, Wendat, Wolastoqiyik) and the Native Alliance of Quebec.

**Eeyou (Cree) entrepreneurs are eligible for all three programs below**: the Non-Refundable Contribution (ABDP), the Term Loan and the Microcredit program for Indigenous women (per SOCCA's website).

## Non-Refundable Contribution — Aboriginal Business Development Program (ABDP)
For starting, acquiring or expanding a business.
- Maximum contribution: **up to $99,999 for a private (individual) project**; up to $250,000 for a community project (brochure).
- Maximum contribution rates by activity (brochure):
  - Preliminary studies (business plan, forecasts, environmental assessments, business or asset valuation): **75%**
  - Capital costs (equipment, inventory, leasehold improvements, rolling stock, commercial buildings, etc.): **40%**
  - Marketing (business cards, lettering, advertising, website, etc.): **60%**
  - Management support (accounting, HR, marketing, management): **75%**

Eligibility:
- Company majority-owned by a First Nation or the Native Alliance of Quebec, or by one or more of their members.
- Project phase: start-up, business acquisition or expansion.
- Down payment / financial resources of **10% to 25%** of project cost.
- Viable, profitable project; good credit rating; experience and training in the field.
- Mandatory full-time involvement in the business (no passive investment).
- No negative impact on competition.
- Excluded sectors: gambling, sex, alcohol, tobacco, marijuana, e-cigarettes, cryptocurrency, etc.

The non-repayable contribution is a **last resort**: all other funding sources must have been considered, and borrowing capacity and down payment must be at their maximum. The amount depends on eligible costs, the applicant's net worth (down payment) and other financing available.

**No costs incurred before approval are eligible.**

## Term Loan
For businesses, organizations and band councils.
- Customized repayable financing for each stage of a company's development.
- Loan amount based on the borrower's repayment capacity; guarantees must reasonably cover the amount borrowed.
- Typical guarantee values: building and land up to 70%; equipment up to 70%; inventory up to 50%; working capital 0%.
- Down payment: **minimum 10% of total project cost.** A transfer of assets may count as down payment.
- Interest rate: competitive with traditional lenders, based on project risk.
- Duration: based on the lifespan of the asset.

## Microcredit — Indigenous Women's Entrepreneurship Program (IWE)
For Indigenous women starting or growing a business. Part-time businesses are eligible. May include a non-repayable contribution.
- IWE loan: maximum **$25,000**; maximum project cost **$75,000**; up to 5 years; advantageous fixed interest rate.
- Loan forgiveness: part of the loan may be forgiven if it is repaid well (conditions apply).
- Eligible costs: start-up costs, employee salaries, working capital, equipment rental or purchase, marketing and promotion, inventory, external professional services, training programs.
