# Eeyou Istchee — Population and demographics (for business plans and market research)

Sources:
- Cree Board of Health and Social Services of James Bay (CBHSSJB), Annual Report 2025–2026, "Population Health Profile" and "Eeyou Istchee Population" pages. Population counts are JBNQA Cree beneficiaries (2025).
- Cree School Board (CSB), Annual Report 2024–2025, Youth Sector and Post-Secondary Student Services pages.

Use these figures for the market section of a business plan (local market size, target market, age groups). Always name the source and year, e.g. "(CBHSSJB Annual Report 2025–26)". Population counts are Cree beneficiaries only; non-Cree residents and workers in the communities aren't included.

## Regional profile (CBHSSJB 2025–26)
- Population growth: about 5,000 in 1976 → 21,320 in 2025.
- Population increase 2021–2025: 6% (same as Quebec, 6%).
- Cree share of the population: 94%.
- Cree speakers: 92% (2025).
- **Young population:** youth under 15 = 26% (Quebec 15%); adults over 65 = 7% (Quebec 22%).
- Fertility rate (2019–2023): 2.4 children per woman (Quebec 1.5).
- Households with more than 1 person per room (2021): 24% (Quebec 1%), a sign of housing shortage and crowding.
- Life expectancy at birth (2021–2023): 75.9 years (Quebec 82.5).
- Diabetes prevalence, adults 20+ (2021): 31%.

What this means for businesses (general observations, not from the report): a large and growing youth market; strong demand for housing, construction and renovation; Cree-language marketing reaches nearly everyone; and health and wellness products and services that fit community needs.

## Post-secondary students supported by CSB (2024–2025)
Region-wide: 492 students (328 college, 136 university, 28 vocational). The report lists 73 graduates (60 college, 8 university, 5 vocational) on its summary page and 59 graduates in its text. There are 9 Master's and 14 Doctoral students. CSB Post-Secondary Student Services has offices in Montreal and Gatineau.
