# Cree Nation Government — Department of Commerce and Industry (DCI): grants for entrepreneurs

Sources: DCI "Funding Grants for Entrepreneurs" infographic (revised December 9, 2025) and DCI "CEAF News" release (December 4, 2025). The full CEAF policy and application form are separate documents in this library.

Contact: Department of Commerce and Industry, Cree Nation Government · 284 Queen Street, Suite 201, Mistissini QC G0W 1C0 · 418-923-2901 · commerce@cngov.ca · www.cngov.ca (Governance structure → Departments → Commerce and Industry) · facebook.com/DCICNG

DCI also offers advisory services, workshops and program support for Cree businesses.

## The three DCI grant programs
| Program | For | Grant |
|---|---|---|
| **CEAF** — Cree Entrepreneurship Assistance Fund | Business start-ups or expansions of existing businesses | Up to **25%** of project cost, max **$150,000** |
| **CREED** — Cree Real Estate Entrepreneurship Development | Start-ups and expansions of businesses in renovations, construction of private homes, and commercial real estate projects | Up to **25%** of project cost, max **$100,000** |
| **CSERT** — Cree Social Economy Round Table | Not-for-profit businesses with a social or cultural component | Up to **75%** of project cost, max **$25,000** |

## CEAF cap increase (news, December 4, 2025)
Effective **October 23, 2025**, the maximum CEAF grant rose from **$100,000 to $150,000**, still capped at 25% of total project costs. It's the first increase since CEAF started in 2011. The higher cap is meant to help Cree-owned businesses invest in equipment, infrastructure and construction as they start, scale or diversify. Updated CEAF policy and application forms are on the DCI webpage.

## Cree Nation Business Registry & Opportunities
An online registry, an initiative of the Grand Council of the Crees (Eeyou Istchee) and the Cree Nation Government. It connects Cree businesses with customers and opportunities in Eeyou Istchee. Businesses can create an account and register their business, browse the business registry and posted opportunities, and see participating communities. More information: www.cngov.ca.
