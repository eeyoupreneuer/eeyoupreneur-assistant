If you are a Cree JBNQA beneficiary who’s employed and ready to advance your career, this program
is designed for you. Funding can help make job-related training more affordable, along with
assistance for travel and other supports where needed.
Here’s how to get started:
Talk to your employer about the training you want and how it supports your career goals.
Collect key details like training dates, provider information, costs, and a short explanation of how it
connects to your role.
Ask your employer to submit an application or contact our team at ASD. We’ll guide you on eligibility
and required documents to move your plan forward.

Tip: The stronger your training plan connects to your job and long-term goals, the easier it is to get
approved.
