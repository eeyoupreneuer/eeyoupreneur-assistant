Research. Innovate. Build Workforce Solutions.
WORKFORCE PROGRAM

The Research and Development Program supports projects that identify workforce needs, develop
new training solutions, and remove barriers to employment in Eeyou Istchee. By investing in research
and innovative service models, this program helps create tools, partnerships, and strategies that
build a stronger labour market and long-term economic resilience.

Partner with Us to Innovate

Do you have an idea for a study, partnership initiative, or employment tool? You can still be involved!
While project funding must be administered through an organization, individuals are encouraged to
collaborate with schools, employers, service providers, or community organizations. ASD can guide
you through eligibility requirements and help shape a strong, impactful proposal.
Next Steps:
Contact ASD early to share your idea, confirm eligibility, and begin building a project that transforms
insight into action.

Eligible
Activities

Initiate strategic partnerships and consolidate networks.
Identify workforce needs and analyze labour market trends.
Promote employment and develop labour market information tools.
Address barriers to employment by designing wrap around career development
and counselling services.
Create innovative skills training programs to increase client employability and foster
labour market integration.

Expected
Results

Improved functioning and development of the Cree labour market leading to
increased skills levels and employment outcomes for Cree clients and reduced
labour shortage for employers.
The Applicant must present a report on the funded activities.

Participants

Duration

For a maximum of 52 weeks.

Expenses

Professional fees at a of maximum $1,000 per day.
Research and development expenses.
Travel and accommodation expenses.
Administration costs must not exceed 15% of the sub-total of the project’s
expenditures before administration costs.

Non-Eligible

Capital costs for the construction or purchase of a building.
Expenses which would have otherwise been incurred by the Applicant.
Capital expenses, such as computers and equipment, not related to the project.
Expenses incurred before the application is submitted.
