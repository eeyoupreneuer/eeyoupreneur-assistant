Youth Initiative
Learn, Earn, and Build Your Future
YOUTH PROGRAM

The Youth Initiative Program helps youth develop life skills and job-ready experience through
innovative placements. Whether students choose part-time work during school or full-time
opportunities, this program offers hands-on learning that builds confidence, strengthens
employability, and supports the transition into the labour market.

Why Organizations Should Participate
APPLICATION DEADLINE (as shown on the ASD website when saved, Nov 2026): 
Employers across Eeyou Istchee can shape the future by providing life-changing opportunities for
youth. ASD supports these placements by funding wages, mandatory employment costs, and
additional expenses directly tied to meaningful work experiences. Extra assistance is also available
for participants with disabilities (up to $3,000), and approved administration costs can be
included.Placements can be part-time during studies (10–20 hours/week) or full-time (35+
hours/week), as long as activities are completed by March 31 of the fiscal year. If you’re ready to
create positive, practical experiences for youth, contact ASD today to confirm eligibility and start
planning your project.

Your Path to Opportunity
ELIGIBLE PARTICIPANTS

Are you between 15 and 30 and looking for work experience to help prepare for your future? The
Youth Initiative Program supports both part-time roles during school and full-time positions so you
can learn while earning. Even if you’re under 16, you can qualify with parental consent.
How It Works:
- Employers apply to ASD for funding to host youth placements.
- You apply for jobs as usual and mention the Youth Initiative Program so employers know ASD can
help them fund your role.
- Part-time positions during school are designed to keep your studies a priority, with no work during
exam periods.
What You Can Do Next:
Start applying for jobs and let employers know they may qualify for support through the Youth
Initiative Program. Prepare any needed documents, like school attendance proof or parental
authorization (if under 16). If you have questions or need help finding placements, reach out to ASD
today. We’re here to help you every step of the way.

Activities that allow students and youth to develop life skills and gain work experience
