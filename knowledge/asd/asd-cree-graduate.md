Cree Graduate
Paid Internships For New Graduates
CAPACITY PROGRAM

This program is designed to help recent Cree graduates transition smoothly into their careers with
structured, full-time internships. By combining financial assistance and meaningful placements, it
ensures new professionals can gain real-world experience while strengthening the workforce of
Eeyou Istchee.

Turn Your Education into Opportunity

APPLICANT COMMITMENT

Maximum of 52 weeks.

You worked hard to earn your diploma or degree—now it’s time to put it to work. This program gives
you a paid, career-relevant placement so you can build professional experience, develop your
network, and gain confidence to thrive in your chosen field. Internships are prioritized within Eeyou
Istchee, but opportunities across Canada are possible if a local placement isn’t available.
Additional Support:
If you need disability-related accommodations, special assistance is available to make your
experience successful.
Next Steps:
- Update your résumé and gather your official transcript.
- Think about the role you want and the skills you hope to build.

Eligible Activities

Activities that:
Assist the graduate in making a successful transition into the labour market in the
chosen field of studies and maintaining employment.
Are related preferably to the field of studies of the graduate throughout the
measure.
Are located in Eeyou Istchee, or elsewhere within Canada, if an internship
opportunity is not available in Eeyou Istchee.

Expected Results

Provide graduates with the opportunity to acquire work experience in their field of
study, increasing employment in Eeyou Istchee
Encourage employers to launch internship projects.

Eligible
Participants

Have graduated in the last 24 months from a College or University program
(diploma, bachelor degree or higher).
Not already be employed full-time in his field of studies.
Not be receiving government benefits during the measure
The participant must provide a résumé and an official transcript.

Duration

Maximum of 52 weeks, with a potential subsidy for part of the salary for the next 3
years under the Job Creation program.

Eligible Expenses

Remuneration of $21.97 per hour, 35 hours a week, for a maximum of $40,000
per year.
Special assistance for persons with disabilities, up to $3,000 per participant.

Non-Eligible
Expenses

Capital costs for the construction or purchase of a building, other than to support
the participation of persons with disabilities
Expenses which would have otherwise been incurred by the Applicant.
Capital expenses, such as computers and equipment, not related to the project.
Expenses incurred before the application is submitted.
