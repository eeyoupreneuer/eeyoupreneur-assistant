YOUTH PROGRAM

The Cooperative Education Placements program connects students with hands-on work
opportunities while completing their studies. By joining forces with schools and employers, this
program creates meaningful learning experiences that prepare youth for the transition from school to
work, helping them gain confidence, employability skills, and career insight.

Take Your First Step
Co-op placements are available for students in Grades 7 to 11 enrolled in Cree School Board managed
educational institutions, typically ages 15–30 (but younger students may be eligible).
How it works:
Your school partners with an employer to create a tailored co-op experience connected to your
studies.
Funding is managed through the school. Students don’t apply for funding directly.
This initiative focuses on learning, mentorship, and safety, not wages.

What you can do next:
Ask your teacher, guidance counselor, or co-op coordinator if placements are available.
Share your interests to help match you to the right opportunity.

Show up ready to learn, ask questions, and gain valuable on-the-job experience.
Tip: If you have a workplace in mind, tell your school—they may be able to build a partnership for
you!
