WORKFORCE PROGRAM

The Support for Persons with Disabilities Program helps remove employment barriers by providing
financial assistance for organizations to hire adults with disabilities and create accessible
workplaces. With adaptable hours and funding for accommodations, this program promotes
independence, skill-building, and equal participation in Eeyou Istchee’s labour market.

Why Organizations Should get Involved

the resources to create meaningful jobs for individuals with disabilities by covering wages and
mandatory employment costs (up to $35,000 per participant each year) and helping fund
accommodations, from accessibility modifications to specialized equipment. Support is also available

What You Can Do Next:
Reach out to ASD or your local office to share the type of work you’re interested in and any barriers
you’ve experienced. If you have an employer in mind, we can help them access this program and set
up the supports you need. Not sure what accommodations would best suit you? We’ll work with you
to explore options that make work safe and successful.
Tip: This program is designed to make employment work for you. Together, we’ll build a plan that fits
your abilities and goals.
