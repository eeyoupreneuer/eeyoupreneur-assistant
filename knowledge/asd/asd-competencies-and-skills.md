WORKFORCE PROGRAM

The Competencies and Skills program helps individuals upgrade their qualifications through
accredited training programs designed to expand employability and career opportunities. By reducing
financial barriers to professional development, this program supports participants on the path to fulltime, sustainable employment and helps create a stronger workforce across Eeyou Istchee.

Strengthen Your Workforce
APPLICATION DEADLINE (as shown on the ASD website when saved, Nov 2026): 
Invest in your future workforce and support the career growth of individuals in your community. The
Competencies and Skills program partners with organizations to fund accredited, job-focused
training that results in recognized credentials and long-term employment. Support can help cover
training delivery, travel, accommodations for remote learning, and specialized equipment. Together,
we can build a skilled and adaptable workforce in Eeyou Istchee. Contact ASD to ensure compliance
and create a strong funding plan.

APPLICANT COMMITMENT

Complete Training Program

Looking for a way to gain in-demand skills and increase your employment options? The
Competencies and Skills program gives you the opportunity to complete accredited training courses
that lead to recognized certifications—without the financial stress. You can apply on your own—no
employer is required.If your pathway includes apprenticeships or specialized trades, make sure you
meet any required prerequisites. Funding can help with tuition and essentials for pursuing your
training, so you can focus on building the career you want.

You may qualify if you are:
At least 18 years old or have a Secondary School Diploma.
Taking or accepted into accredited training that increases employability and leads to full-time
employment.
Meeting diploma and industry prerequisites for apprenticeship programs.
Here’s how to get started:
Choose an accredited program that matches your career goals.
Gather your training details: dates, costs, location, and any required equipment.

If you need travel, lodging, or childcare support, prepare the documentation.
Submit your application to ASD or contact us for guidance to build a strong plan.
Tip: Applications with a clear link between your training and long-term employment are more likely to
be approved.

Eligible
Activities

Accredited training activities that increase the employability of participants and lead to
regular full-time employment.

Expected
Results

Increased employability of participants, leading to sustainable employment.

Participants

Must be at least 18 years of age or hold a Secondary School Diploma.
For apprenticeships, participants must have the necessary diploma and prerequisites
as recognized by regulations of the industry.

Duration

Until completion of the training program.

Expenses

Training Delivery:
Training expenses at a maximum of $1,000 per day.
A maximum annual contribution of $35,000 per participant.
Safety and specialized equipment.
Travel expenses in accordance with CNG’s Business Travel Policy.
Special assistance for persons with disabilities, up to $3,000 per participant.
Administration costs must not exceed 15% of the sub-total of the project’s
expenditure before administration costs.
Training Allowance:
Single: $428.50
Amount increases with dependents

Lodging at the location of training, if different from community of residence:
Less than 4 weeks: In accordance with CNG’s Business Travel Policy.
More than 4 weeks: Maximum of $280 per week
Childcare:
Childcare Center or School Age Program: $8.70 per day with a maximum of $43.50 per day
for all dependents, or adjusted to the provincial rate.
Private Childcare: Maximum of $250 per week based on $50 per day per dependent, with
a proof that the dependent cannot attend a Childcare Center due to lack of space. A
babysitter must be at least 18 years old. The participant’s spouse or immediate siblings of
the dependent are not eligible.
