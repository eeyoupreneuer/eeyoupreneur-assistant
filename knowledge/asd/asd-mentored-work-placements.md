Your First Step Into the Workplace
ELIGIBLE PARTICIPANTS

If you’re between 15 and 30, not in school, and looking for a chance to gain work experience, this
program is for you. A mentored work placement offers more than a job—it’s an opportunity to develop
practical skills, earn a paycheck, and work with someone who will guide and support you on the job.

Youth NOT in school and
unemployed or underemployed.
Youth aged 15 to 30

This experience can put you on the path to future education or a long-term career.
How It Works:
You’ll join a work placement where you learn by doing, supported by a mentor who cares about your
growth. Employers receive funding to help with wages and other costs, making it possible for them to
offer you this experience. Depending on your needs, other supports for training, mobility, dependent
care, or disability accommodations may also be available.
What to Do Next:
Reach out to your local ASD office or an organization offering placements. Share what type of work
interests you and the skills you want to build. If you already have an employer in mind, ask if they can
host you through this program. Be ready to show up, commit, learn, and make the most of your
mentorship. Even if your résumé is short, this program exists to help you grow your experience and
your confidence.

Expenses

Participant wages at the following maximum hourly rate of $17.30.
Rates for wage subsidies may be subject to change.
Mandatory employment related costs.
Training costs for activities that support the acquisition of skills required for work
Other necessary costs directly related to a work placement, up to a maximum of
$300 per participant.
Special assistance for persons with disabilities, up to $3,000 per participant.
Travel expenses in accordance with CNG’s Business Travel Policy but not exceeding
the guidelines specified in the Treasury Board of Canada Secretariat’s Travel
Dependent care for participants (documentation is required including a description
of the type of arrangements available in the community).
Administration costs must not exceed 15% of the sub-total amount of the project’s
expenditures before administration costs.

Non-Eligible

participation of persons with disabilities.

Office infrastructure or equipment purchases such as computers and software.
Expenses incurred before the application is submitted.
Development and production of communication and information products.
