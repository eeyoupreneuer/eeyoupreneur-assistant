YOUTH PROGRAM

The Summer Work Experience Program gives secondary and postsecondary students in Eeyou
Istchee the chance to turn summer into opportunity. By connecting youth with paid work placements,
this program helps build confidence, develop real-world skills, and strengthen the pathway from
school to meaningful employment. Together, we are preparing the next generation for success and
creating a stronger local economy.

Employers: Partner and Grow Your Workforce
Looking to support local talent and strengthen your team? With ASD wage subsidies, you can create
summer placements for Cree youth while reducing payroll costs. Our funding can cover wages,
mandatory employment-related costs, and also includes support for participants with disabilities,
making it easier than ever to provide meaningful positions.If your organization can offer a minimum of
80 hours of paid work within the fiscal year, you may qualify for funding. This is your opportunity to
invest in youth, build capacity, and make a lasting impact in Eeyou Istchee.

How it Works
Apatisiiwin Skills Development assists local employers by offsetting the cost of hiring students. This
ensures more summer jobs are available while giving you the chance to learn and earn.
When you apply for a position, let the employer know they can receive wage subsidies through ASD.
It is a simple way to encourage hiring and create opportunities that benefit everyone.

Eligibility Snapshot
This program is for students aged 15 to 30 who were enrolled as full-time students last year and
intend to return to school. Placements must provide at least 80 paid hours and be completed within
the fiscal year. Employers must be located in Eeyou Istchee and able to offer meaningful work
experience.
Funding helps cover wages and related costs and includes additional support for individuals with
disabilities.
