YOUTH PROGRAM

The Science and Technology Program gives youth opportunities to learn, create, and imagine what’s
possible in the world of science, technology, engineering, and math (STEM). Through engaging
activities like science camps, tech workshops, conferences, and coding clubs (often connected to
traditional Indigenous knowledge), students build future-ready skills such as teamwork, critical
thinking, and problem-solving.

Bring STEM Learning to Life
