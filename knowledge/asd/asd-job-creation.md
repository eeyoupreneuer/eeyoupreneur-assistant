Invest In People. Invest In The Future.
WORKFORCE PROGRAM

The Job Creation Program empowers organizations to create sustainable, full-time positions for
unemployed individuals, helping them gain experience, build skills, and succeed in the labour market.
This program strengthens community capacity by supporting ongoing employment, not short-term
placements.

Partner with Us to Build the Future
APPLICATION DEADLINE (as shown on the ASD website when saved, Nov 2026): 
The Job Creation Program allows your organization to access wage subsidies and additional
supports to create long-term jobs. Projects run for a minimum of 52 weeks and can be renewed for
two additional years, with cost-sharing adjusted each year.Funding can assist with wages, essential
employment costs, job-specific training, travel needs, and workplace accommodations for
participants with disabilities. By partnering with ASD, you help develop skilled talent while
strengthening Eeyou Istchee’s economic foundation. Connect with us today to confirm eligibility and
start building lasting opportunities.

APPLICANT COMMITMENT

Start Your Career with Confidence

Minimum of 52 weeks

Looking for a chance to gain real work experience and kickstart your career? The Job Creation
Program helps you step into a full-time job designed to build your skills, knowledge, and confidence.
These positions give you the chance to work, learn, and grow—while employers prepare pathways
for long-term success.

ELIGIBLE PARTICIPANTS

Next Steps:
Get your résumé ready and reach out to ASD for details on current opportunities and eligibility. We’ll
work with organizations to match you to roles that provide meaningful experience and a foundation
for your future. This is an opportunity to turn unemployment into empowerment.

Eligible Activities

Job creation that will improve the employability of participants and facilitate their
successful integration into the labour market.

Increased employment opportunities and enhancement of skills for participants.
Lead to regular full-time employment.
Encourage employers to hire and train new personnel in a specific job position.

Eligible
Participants

Must be unemployed
Must be at least 18 years of age or hold a Secondary School diploma
For apprenticeships, participants must have the necessary diploma and
prerequisites as recognized by regulation of the industry.

Duration

For a minimum of 52 weeks, renewable upon reapplication for a second and third
year.
Hours per week in accordance with federal and/or provincial regulations.
