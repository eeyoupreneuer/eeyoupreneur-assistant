Cree Bursary
Your Dreams Deserve Every Change
CAPACITY PROGRAM

The Cree Bursary Program supports JBNQA Cree beneficiaries who are enrolled full-time in postsecondary, vocational, or accredited certificate programs. This $1,000 bursary, offered each Fall and
Winter semester, helps students stay focused on their education and future careers in Eeyou Istchee.

Why this Program Matters
Education is the foundation for future opportunities. The Cree Bursary Program makes your academic
journey more affordable by providing financial relief where it matters most. Helping you reduce costs

For Students: How it Works

The Cree Bursary provides $1,000 per eligible student for each Fall and Winter semester. Students

When to Apply:
-Apply after completing 30 days in your semester, once ASD announces the application period is
open (shared via ASD website, radio, social media, and email).
-Submit your application before the end of that semester to qualify.
What You’ll Need:
- A current letter of enrolment
- Completed ASD application form
- Banking information (such as a void cheque for new applicants)
Tip: Apply early once the application period opens to ensure on-time processing.
