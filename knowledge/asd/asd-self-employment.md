Self- Employment
From Idea To Independence.
WORKFORCE PROGRAM

The Self-Employment Program supports JBNQA beneficiaries who are ready to turn a business idea
into full-time work. With structured funding, cost-sharing, and guidance, this program helps ensure
your business plan becomes more than a dream—it becomes your future and contributes to Eeyou
Istchee’s economic growth.

Why Choose Self-Employment
APPLICATION DEADLINE (as shown on the ASD website when saved, Nov 2026): 
Self-employment is more than a job—it’s a career you own and control. Through this program, ASD
helps individuals start viable, permanent businesses that create at least 35 hours of weekly work.
Funding can support owner draws and start-up costs for up to three years, combined with increasing
cost-sharing from the participant to ensure long-term sustainability.Your business must have a
realistic chance of becoming permanent and should strengthen the local labour market by creating
balance between private and public sectors. With the right plan, this is your opportunity to move from
unemployment to owning your future.

APPLICANT COMMITMENT

How it Works

Maximum of 52 weeks

The process begins with a strong business plan that clearly shows what your business will do, who it
serves, how it will operate as a permanent full-time job, and why it is viable. You’ll also need basic
business structures in place: a registered business number, permits or licenses where required, and a
business bank account.

ELIGIBLE PARTICIPANTS

Funding provides cost-shared assistance for up to three years—50% in year one, 60% in year two,
and 70% in year three—with owner draws capped and focused on direct business costs. Once
approved, your role as the applicant and owner is to launch, track, and manage your business
according to your plan. ASD is here to guide you throughout the process to make sure you stay on
track.
If you’re unsure about your eligibility or need help strengthening your proposal, contact ASD early—
we can prevent delays and help you build a solid plan.

Ready to Start Your Business?
If you are at least 18, unemployed, and a JBNQA beneficiary with experience or training for your
chosen field, this program can help you transition into self-employment. Preparing your plan and
documents is your first step toward independence. Connect with ASD to confirm eligibility and take
the first steps toward your future today.
