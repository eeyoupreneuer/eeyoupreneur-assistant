# Eeyoupreneur — services and contact

Last checked: November 2026

Eeyoupreneur is an Indigenous-owned and operated business consultancy and design studio based in Eeyou Istchee, founded by Samuel McLeod (ᓵᒥᔭᓪ ᒦᑲᓚᑦ) of Waskaganish. All services are delivered in community.

## Services
- Business plan development
- Funding application support
- Client proposals
- Graphic design and branding
- Marketing and websites
- Workshops and facilitation on economic development
